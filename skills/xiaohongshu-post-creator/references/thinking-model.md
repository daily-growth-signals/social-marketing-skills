# Thinking model (learn methods, do not imitate styles)

Borrow **reasoning**, not viral template packs. Formulaic notes get detected as repetitive low-quality content.

## Layer A — Evidence (SignalDig)

- Only claim “小红书上现在…” from a terminal SignalDig result.
- Observation vs inference stay separate.
- Do not claim you understood image pixels/OCR unless that content is in the evidence.
- Metrics only as returned; do not translate other platforms’ metrics.
- State sample size; never call a small page “全网规律.”
- Failed/empty research → no fake “基于笔记” claims.

## Layer B — Judgment (angle craft)

On **this retrieval only**:

1. Which angles already saturate titles/descriptions?
2. Which specific details (not hype words) appear in stronger notes—if metrics exist?
3. What honest gap can **this user** fill (local, process, failure, constraint)?
4. What would be dishonest exaggeration given the sample?
5. If mimicking “my account’s pacing,” only from a user-supplied profile/posts fetch—paraphrase pacing, never copy notes.

No permanent “爆款开头 20 式” menu. No fixed carousel page count as a brand promise.

## Layer C — Draft discipline (Xiaohongshu ≠ technical doc)

**Honesty (STC-like, always on):**

- No invented results, prices, or “我实测” without user input → `【待填】`.
- Cut empty AI filler and fake urgency that adds no information.
- Keep uncertainty when the sample is thin.
- Agent-facing delivery stays short and structured (见 delivery-contract).

**Xiaohongshu voice (do not over-apply STC):**

- “你/我”、场景感和口语节奏 are normal here.
- Hooks are allowed when they are **specific to this topic and sample**, not from a shared hook library.
- Do not strip all emotion into dry manual tone.
- Do not force “一句不超过 40 字” on story notes.

**Not a Zhihu converter model:** do not force long academic sections or “建立权威履历” templates when the user gave no credentials.

## Anti-fingerprint rule

Forbidden as the product center:

- Shared hook banks, emoji scripts, or “封面必杀句”
- Verbatim reuse of other notes
- Same 3-page/10-page skeleton for every topic
- Auto-publish / login automation disguised as “marketing skill”

Outline and wording must follow **this round’s** notes and the user’s real gaps so mass cloning is hard.
