# Thinking model (learn methods, do not imitate styles)

This Skill borrows **reasoning patterns** from strong writing/editing skills. It does **not** copy word banks, catchphrase packs, fixed hook menus, or a named author’s voice. Those create fingerprintable, repetitive posts that platforms and readers treat as spam.

## Layer A — Evidence (SignalDig)

- Only claim “知乎上现在…” from a terminal SignalDig result.
- Separate **observation** (in the payload) from **inference** (your judgment).
- Titles/excerpts ≠ full answer bodies unless the body is present.
- Missing metrics are unknown, not zero. No fabricated numbers.
- Empty or failed research → say so; do not draft as if research succeeded.

## Layer B — Judgment (argument craft)

Use these questions on **this retrieval only**. Explain in plain language tied to items.

1. **Worth writing now?** Attention vs answer thinness vs dispute vs staleness—only from observed fields.
2. **What is already covered?** List angles present in the sample.
3. **Where is the gap?** Thin, outdated, one-sided, or contested spots.
4. **Who pays / who benefits / under what premise?** (interest and boundary thinking—not a slang pack)
5. **Case vs pattern?** Do not promote a single anecdote in the sample to “everyone.”
6. **Identity as evidence:** mention credentials only when the user supplied firsthand material; otherwise `【待填】`. Identity is a source of facts, not a costume.
7. **Counterview + boundary:** what opposing take is reasonable, and when your conclusion does not apply.

Do not score with a secret permanent formula. Do not force a canned “three genres × hooks × tones” menu.

## Layer C — Draft discipline (Zhihu reader + clear Chinese)

**Zhihu reader expectations (editorial thinking, not templates):**

- Prefer depth, checkable claims, low hard-sell.
- Title states the judgment or unique angle.
- Early paragraphs state the conclusion from this analysis.
- Structure follows **this round’s** gaps and disputes, so two topics rarely share the same skeleton.

**Clear Chinese for facts and Agent delivery (STC-like discipline):**

- Lead with conclusion, then evidence.
- One sentence, one idea for factual claims; cut empty courtesy and filler.
- Degree words (“显著、大量”) → numbers or concrete facts from evidence/user; else `【待填】`.
- Keep uncertainty words (“可能、通常”) when the evidence is partial.
- One meaning, one term inside a single draft.
- Do **not** apply technical-doc bans on all rhetorical devices: Zhihu may use contrast or longer paragraphs when they carry real argument—never as empty slogan.

## Anti-fingerprint rule

Forbidden as the product center:

- Permanent hook/title/PS catalogs
- Mandatory catchphrase substitution tables
- Clone-another-creator voice packs
- Batch-friendly skeletons that only swap nouns

Every draft should be hard to reuse as a template for unrelated topics because its outline came from **this sample’s** coverage and gaps.
